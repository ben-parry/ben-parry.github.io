I’m Benjamin Parry. I run The Toronto Society, a new home for learning, research, and culture. We host lectures, reading groups, debates and more.

For the last few years I have been interested in human judgement and how it leads to success in business ventures, political leadership and moral reform—something that seems particularly pressing in the current moment. As part of that I’ve explored complexity science, theology, and the stories of successful investors and company builders. I’ve written about what I’ve learned. The Society is a meeting place to make these lessons concrete by supporting a community of excellence that pursues original, wise work. As the old Toronto motto puts it: Industry. Intelligence. Integrity.

My career more broadly has been about making complex things clear so we can build in a way that is more beautiful and effective. Most recently, before the Toronto Society, that meant co-founding Build Canada, where I led policy. Our recommendations were read widely within the Canadian federal government and shaped billions of dollars of spending to help make Canada the most prosperous nation on earth. Before that I worked at Tim Hortons, where I designed, built and ran the largest loyalty program in Canada, including setting up the organisation’s data science team.

I grew up in London , before heading to Philadelphia  for university. Along the way I lived for 6 months in 佛山  and a year in NYC . I’m grateful to now be based in Toronto .

I’m active on X and I always enjoy connecting with like-minded people.
